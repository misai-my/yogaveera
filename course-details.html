Welcome to the Yoga Veera Online Course!

It is wonderful to see you here with the willingness to become a Yoga Veera. In this course you will be trained to offer simple yet powerful practices to the people around you, both in person and online.

The course consists of multiple sections which you can go through at your own pace. We recommend you to go through it in the order it is presented.

We wish you a lot of fun with this course and are happy to see your first Yoga Veera session soon!

With joy,

Isha volunteers :)

Requirements

No requirements or prerequisites are needed to go through this course! If you know someone that is interested in offering powerful yogic practices to others, they are more then welcome to join!
